# Prompt 4 — Three thumbnail concepts

---
Create 3 thumbnail concepts for "<title>" aiming for a high click-through rate. For each one give: the layout
(versus | badge | checklist | headline), the text (4 words or fewer), the face expression (calm | shout | smirk),
and why it gets clicks. Write them as `thumbs.json` (see `examples/thumbs.json`), then run
`python3 ss.py thumbs <slug> --spec thumbs.json` and check `feed_preview_phone-size.png`.

Rules: 4 words or fewer · one big face · strong contrast (dark backgrounds pop on YouTube's light feed) ·
nothing in the bottom-right corner (the duration badge sits there) · readable at phone size ·
no weapons pointed at heads, no gore (bad for monetisation).
Recommend which two to A/B with YouTube's Test & Compare.
